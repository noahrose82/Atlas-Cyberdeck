# Funnel Metrics

Track:

```text
Landing page visits
        ↓
Email signup
        ↓
Kickstarter click
        ↓
Kickstarter follow
        ↓
Launch pledge
```

Calculate:

```text
Email signup rate
= email signups / landing-page visitors

Kickstarter follow rate
= Kickstarter followers / landing-page visitors

Follower-to-backer rate
= backers from followers / Kickstarter followers

Overall visitor-to-backer rate
= total backers / landing-page visitors
```

Do not assign a "good" conversion rate before actual Atlas data exists.

Build Atlas's own baseline.
