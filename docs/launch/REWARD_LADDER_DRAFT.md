# Important Rule

Do not promise:

- unlimited personal support;
- custom engineering;
- custom device compatibility;
- every future Atlas feature;
- permanent third-party services;
- vague "everything forever" access.

Core runtime safety remains available regardless of future Free / Pro monetization.
