# Why $15,000 Is a Good Initial Target

It is large enough to represent a real product-completion budget but small enough that Atlas does not need a massive pre-existing audience to have a plausible chance of funding.

Because Kickstarter is all-or-nothing, setting the goal higher than the project actually requires adds campaign risk.

The campaign can still raise beyond the goal.

## Recommended — $15,000

Best current balance.


## Expanded — $20,000

```text

Approx. net       $18,000
```

Could support additional device testing and more 1.0 engineering runway, but requires significantly more backers.

# What Is Not Included Yet

Do not lock these until the campaign economics are validated:

- income-tax reserve;
- final legal/accounting cost;
- paid advertising;
- contractor design/video work;
- physical merchandise;
- lifetime-license economics;
- App Store / Play Store commercial pricing;
- post-1.0 cloud-service costs.

# Funding Goal Gate

Before publishing **$15,000**, validate:

```text
1. 1.0 delivery scope              LOCKED
2. Reward tiers                    LOCKED
3. Mailing-list size               MEASURED
4. Expected first-day backers      ESTIMATED
5. Average pledge                  MODELED
6. Legal/tax assumptions           REVIEWED
7. Delivery schedule               LOCKED
8. Campaign preview                REVIEWED
```

If audience validation is weak, do not simply lower the goal below what is needed to deliver. Build the audience first.
