# Atlas Cyberdeck — Kickstarter Pre-Launch Page Plan

This file reflects Kickstarter functionality checked during the August 2026 planning phase. Re-verify platform details immediately before publishing.

---

# What Kickstarter Currently Provides

A Kickstarter pre-launch page can display:

- project title;
- subtitle;
- project image;
- category;
- location;
- editable pre-launch description;
- creator identity;
- pre-launch updates.

Potential backers can choose **Notify me on launch**.

Kickstarter sends those followers a launch notification when the campaign goes live.

The platform currently recommends setting up and sharing a pre-launch page at least **one week** before the intended launch.

Atlas should use substantially more than one week if audience validation is still underway.

# Recommended Atlas Pre-Launch Page

## Title

**Atlas Cyberdeck**

## Subtitle

**A real Linux workstation in your pocket. No root required.**

## Image

Use:

```text
docs/images/atlas-cyberdeck-social-card.png
```

or a Kickstarter-specific crop based on the same real-device visuals.

## Category

Choose the most accurate available technology category/subcategory during project setup.

Do not select a category merely because it appears more popular.

## Description

> Atlas Cyberdeck is a native Android cyberdeck platform with an independent Atlas shell and a real Ubuntu ARM64 workspace powered by PRoot. The current alpha already runs Ubuntu 24.04.4 LTS, Linux package tools, Python, runtime safety, Safe Mode, and controlled recovery on real Android hardware — without requiring Android root. Follow the project to be notified when the Kickstarter campaign launches.

# Pre-Launch Updates

Kickstarter currently allows creators to publish updates during pre-launch, and followers receive email notifications when those updates are published.

Use this sparingly.

Recommended Atlas sequence:

## Update 1 — It Runs

Show:

- Android phone;
- Ubuntu version;
- `aarch64`;
- Python.

Message:

> The hardest part of Atlas was proving a real Linux runtime could be integrated into the product experience. Here is the current build doing exactly that.

## Update 2 — Why Atlas Has Safe Mode

Show:

- Safe Mode;
- Recovery Mode.

Message:

> Linux package and runtime failures happen. Atlas does not hide them. Here is how the runtime circuit breaker and controlled recovery flow work.

## Update 3 — What 1.0 Means

Explain:

- what Kickstarter funds;
- what 1.0 includes;
- what is intentionally future work.

## Update 4 — Launch Window

Use only when the campaign is actually ready.

Do not announce a launch date that the campaign cannot meet.

# Followers

Kickstarter currently starts displaying the follower count publicly once at least 10 people have opted to be notified.

Kickstarter's own promotion guidance recommends identifying at least 10 potential backers who can follow the page and support immediately at launch.

For Atlas, 10 followers is only a platform baseline.

The internal Atlas target should be much higher because the working goal is `$15,000`.

---

# Atlas Internal Follower Targets

```text
50 followers     → proof of interest
150 followers    → viable signal
250+ followers   → preferred pre-launch position
```

These are Atlas planning gates, not Kickstarter benchmarks or guarantees.

---

# Follower Communication

Kickstarter introduced follower-only updates across the project lifecycle in 2026.

Use them for meaningful moments rather than constant posting.

Atlas should avoid duplicating Kickstarter's automatic launch / closing notifications with unnecessary messages.

---

# Important Platform Note

Kickstarter launch remains a manual action.

Do not build a launch-day plan that assumes Kickstarter can automatically launch the campaign at a scheduled time.
