# Atlas Labs Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Atlas Labs is committed to maintaining a respectful, technically focused, and constructive environment for people who participate in Atlas Cyberdeck development.

Contributors, users, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected to treat one another professionally.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- communicating respectfully;
- giving technical feedback constructively;
- accepting reasonable disagreement;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- asking questions in good faith;
- helping others understand the project;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting contributor time and boundaries;
- keeping discussions relevant to the project;
- supporting an environment where people can learn and contribute.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment;
- discrimination;
- threats;
- personal attacks;
- intimidation;
- stalking;
- deliberate humiliation;
- abusive or demeaning language;
- sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- repeated disruption of technical discussions;
- deliberately hostile behavior;
- retaliation against someone who reports misconduct.

---

## Technical Disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is acceptable.

Disrespect is not.

It is appropriate to say:

> This implementation bypasses the runtime safety boundary and should be redesigned.

It is not appropriate to attack the person who wrote it.

Reviews should remain centered on:

- correctness;
- architecture;
- safety;
- maintainability;
- performance;
- testing;
- product direction.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and project-related interactions, including:

- issues;
- pull requests;
- discussions;
- code reviews;
- repository comments;
- community spaces operated for Atlas Labs;
- public interactions where someone is representing Atlas Labs or Atlas Cyberdeck.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Actions may include:

- requesting a change in behavior;
- editing or removing inappropriate content;
- closing discussions;
- rejecting contributions;
- temporarily restricting participation;
- permanently restricting participation in severe or repeated cases.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on protecting a productive project environment.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or observe conduct that violates this policy, report it privately to the project owner or through an available private project contact method.

Reports should include enough context to understand what occurred.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as practical.

---

## Good-Faith Participation

Atlas Labs welcomes disagreement, bug reports, criticism, and alternative technical approaches when they are offered constructively.

This Code of Conduct is intended to support serious collaboration, not suppress legitimate technical discussion.

---

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is written specifically for Atlas Labs and Atlas Cyberdeck and is informed by common open-source community standards.
